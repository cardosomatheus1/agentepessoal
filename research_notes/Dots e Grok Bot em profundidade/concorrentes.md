# Competitive landscape: always-on personal AI agents rivalling OpenAI Dots and xAI Grok Bot (as of 2026-10-08)

Context used for comparison (from coverage of the reference products, not the focus here): Grok Bot beta started 2026-08-11 and Dots was announced at OpenAI DevDay on 2026-09-29 — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents); [javiersaezhurtado.com](https://javiersaezhurtado.com/en/articles/grok-bot-vs-openai-dots/). Dots: one Linux computer and Chrome browser per dot, from $100/mo, channels ChatGPT app/Slack/Teams, voice calls, Codex delegation, self-scheduled follow-ups. Grok Bot: built by Cursor, one cloud computer per user, from $20/mo, app + Slack, about 50 bots, six-bot group chats, a separate review model for risky actions, screen-recording-to-skill, and connector tokens kept off the bot's machine — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents).

## Meta Muse: launch date, what it does, channels, proactivity, memory, actions, persona, pricing, availability (Brazil?)

### Takeaway
Muse launched on 2026-09-08 as a consumer-first agent: US-only (Canada reportedly added later), free tier plus $20/$100 plans. It works through an app, the web and **WhatsApp**, and is personalized (name, avatar, style). It runs in a per-user cloud VM with a separate permission agent called "Sentinel", and every purchase requires approval with single-use card payments. Features Dots/Grok Bot lack: a WhatsApp channel, a free tier, a forwardable agent email address, a live video avatar, glasses and wake-word access, a dedicated hardware device, and memory stored as editable text files. There is **no Brazil launch date**.

- Every purchase needs approval with exact details and is paid with a **single-use Stripe Link card**. A separate agent approves every connector action and outbound request — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents).

- **Amazon blocked Muse** on 2026-09-21 ("unauthorized AI agent"). Amazon said the agent did not identify itself and appeared to capture and store customer credentials. Meta replied that Muse has no visibility into passwords or payment methods and that credentials sit in secure storage — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_(AI_agent)), citing [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-blocks-metas-muse-ai-assistant-in-new-standoff-over-agentic-shopping/); [CNN 09-28](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/28/tech/meta-muse-ai-agents-amazon).

**Security architecture**
- **Muse Secure VM:** a "dedicated, secure computer with its own browser". Muse cannot see passwords or payment methods, and data does not go to Meta ad systems. A separate **Sentinel** agent runs on the same VM, isolated at the system level. TechCrunch notes these claims need expert review — [TechCrunch 09-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/meta-debuts-its-muse-ai-agent-will-consumers-trust-it/).
- Sentinel controls which services the agent can reach and **separates read from write access**. The user can watch the agent's browser live — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_(AI_agent)).
- Queries are used for training by default (opt-out). Meta promised a version "that cannot view a user's workspace" before the end of 2026 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_(AI_agent)).

**Pricing**
- Free to start, but a **payment card is required**. Paid plans are Power ($20/mo) and Maximum ($100/mo). A usage meter warns the user when free usage runs out — [TechCrunch 09-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/meta-debuts-its-muse-ai-agent-will-consumers-trust-it/).
- No ads. Meta expects to earn a small fee on transactions — [TechCrunch 09-23](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/everything-new-coming-to-metas-ai-agent-muse/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_(AI_agent)). Meta does not say what a "Muse token" measures — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents).

### Inferences
- Copyable for a self-hosted agent: (1) WhatsApp as the primary chat channel, which matters for Brazil; (2) a dedicated agent email inbox that users can forward or CC to; (3) a separate permission/approval process, a "Sentinel" equivalent, that splits read and write scopes per connector and gates every outbound request; (4) single-use virtual cards per purchase; (5) memory as human-editable text files; (6) an activity log showing both past and planned actions; (7) a configurable persona (name, avatar, tone); (8) a usage meter with warnings.
- The Amazon block shows a risk for any browser-driving agent: sites may detect and ban unidentified agents. Preferring APIs and identifying the agent are the safer default.
- Muse's absence from Brazil, despite WhatsApp's reach there, is an opening for a self-hosted WhatsApp agent in PT-BR.

### Gaps
- No primary Meta Newsroom confirmation of the Canada expansion. No Instagram or Messenger channel was found in any source.
- How Muse notifies users (push vs WhatsApp messages) and handles 2FA/OTP prompts is undocumented in the sources found.
- The paid-tier limits (what "Power" and "Maximum" unlock) were not detailed. The 09-23 TechCrunch piece describes no paid tiers, which suggests the emphasis shifted to free plus transaction fees (unconfirmed).
- Ship dates for the email address, avatar, Mac control and glasses were all "soon"; none was confirmed live as of 10-08.

## Google Gemini Spark: launch date, background tasks, triggers, Gmail/Calendar/Android integration, phone-level actions, proactivity, pricing, availability (Brazil?)

### Takeaway
Spark was announced at I/O on 2026-05-19 and runs 24/7 on Google Cloud VMs. Its rollout went from US Ultra beta to US AI Pro (July) to more than 160 countries including **Brazil (2026-07-30/31)**, on AI Pro (R$96.99/mo) and Ultra (R$779.90/mo). Its standouts relative to Dots/Grok Bot: **event-based triggers** alongside schedules, up to 15 parallel tasks, reusable "Skills" (including learning the user's writing style), deep Workspace data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Keep, Tasks), Chrome "Auto Browse", and a planned Android "Halo" status surface. Texting Spark, sub-agents and Halo were announced but are **not confirmed live**.

## Other notable always-on/background agents in 2026 and standout features

### Takeaway
Most rivals use the same pattern: a cloud VM per task or user, schedule and event triggers, and an approval step. Differentiators are channel (Manus on Telegram), triggers (Copilot Cowork reacts to email and Teams messages; Perplexity Computer to Slack, Gmail and GitHub), and ephemeral vs persistent machines.

### Cited Findings
- **Microsoft Copilot Cowork:** triggered by schedule, **email or Teams message**. A temporary workspace is deleted after each task. $30/user/mo license plus usage — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents).
- **Anthropic Claude:** cloud Cowork sessions, scheduled tasks and Claude Code routines. Steps that need local files or apps require the laptop. From $20/mo — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents). Claude Cowork, released January 2026, is a desktop agent working in local folders — [getsliq comparison](https://getsliq.com/blog/manus-vs-claude-cowork-vs-perplexity-computer) (secondary).
- **Perplexity Computer** (late Feb 2026): a cloud orchestrator coordinating 19 models on background projects. It uses a fresh cloud machine per task or "Automation" and is triggered from **Slack, Gmail or GitHub**. Pro $20/mo plus credits — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents); [getsliq](https://getsliq.com/blog/manus-vs-claude-cowork-vs-perplexity-computer).
- **Manus** (Meta-owned since Dec 2025): personal agents **in Telegram since 2026-02-16**, set up by QR-code scan. WhatsApp, LINE, Slack and Discord were promised "very soon" — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/02/16/manus-launches-personal-ai-agents-telegram-messaging-apps-come/); [Silicon Republic](https://www.siliconrepublic.com/machines/manus-ai-agents-meta-china-telegram-whatsapp). - The open-source/self-hosted "OpenClaw" and "Hermes" appear in personal-agent comparisons — [aitooltier](https://aitooltier.com/categories/ai-personal-agents) (not fetched). ### Inferences
- An ephemeral per-task sandbox (Copilot, Perplexity, Manus) plus optional persistent state is a cheap, safe architecture for a self-hosted agent.

### Gaps

## Cross-cutting patterns: notifications, approvals, passwords/2FA, background execution, phone/messaging channels, learning routines

### Takeaway
The 2026 consensus design: a per-user or per-task cloud VM with its own browser; opt-in connectors with read/write separation; human approval for money, outbound messages and bookings, often enforced by a **separate model or agent** (Grok Bot's review model, Muse's Sentinel); drafts by default; scoped payment credentials (single-use cards); activity logs; and memory or skills that learn preferences. Messaging-native channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, email-to-agent) are the main battleground. Voice calls exist (Dots, Muse beta) but carry trust issues.

### Cited Findings
- **Approval:** Spark asks before spending money or sending emails — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/). Muse requires approval for every purchase with exact details, and a separate agent approves connector actions and outbound requests. Grok Bot uses a separate review model for risky actions. Dots buys with a saved card after approval — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents).
- **Credentials:** Muse claims it never sees passwords or payment methods, holds credentials in secure storage, and plans 1Password integration — [TechCrunch 09-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/meta-debuts-its-muse-ai-agent-will-consumers-trust-it/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_(AI_agent)). Grok Bot keeps sign-in tokens on Cursor servers outside the bot's computer — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents). Amazon accused Muse of capturing credentials — [Wikipedia/GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-blocks-metas-muse-ai-assistant-in-new-standoff-over-agentic-shopping/).
- **Background execution:** Spark uses Google Cloud VMs and needs the device awake only for local Chrome tasks. Muse uses Secure VMs. Dots gives each dot a Linux computer plus Chrome — [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents); [TechCrunch 09-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/meta-debuts-its-muse-ai-agent-will-consumers-trust-it/).
- **Visibility and notification surfaces:** Muse lets the user watch the agent's browser live and keeps an activity log of past and planned actions. Android Halo shows agent progress at the top of the screen — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_(AI_agent)); [AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents); [Google on X](https://x.com/Google/status/2056792679887827437).
